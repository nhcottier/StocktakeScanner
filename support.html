Common Questions

What happens if we find more of a product after it has already been counted?

Go back to the saved row in the app and tap the product that needs updating. From there you can adjust the quantity and save the updated count.

Step 1

Open the stocktake

Choose the row, shelf area, or stocktake you were working in.

Step 2

Tap the saved product row

Find the product that needs changing, then tap the row to open the edit screen.

Step 3

Adjust the count and save

Update the quantity, bin location, notes, or product details, then tap Save.

  • The camera will not open: check iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Camera, then allow camera access for Stocktake.
  • The wrong text was read: tap Retake, or edit the product code and description before saving.
  • Large quantities: tap the quantity number and type the count directly with the keyboard.
  • Exporting: use the export button on a stocktake to create an Excel workbook and share it with Mail, Files, or another app.
  • iCloud backup: when iCloud Documents is available, the app writes a backup to the user’s iCloud Documents folder.
